## 1. The A.R.P.A. method (this is what outreach-scripts.md §9 means by "never argue, always A.R.P.A.")

Every objection gets four moves, in order, in under 45 seconds — then the conversation goes back to them with a question.

- **A — Acknowledge.** Find the legitimate core and agree with it out loud. An objection validated stops being a wall and becomes a topic.
- **R — Reframe.** Move the comparison to the true alternative. The competitor is almost never another product; it is the silence at 23h, the personal phone in one pocket, the status quo.
- **P — Proof.** Their numbers from discovery, the live demo, the written contract clauses, the 30-day guarantee. Never invented clients, never borrowed logos — we have none, and we say so (pricing-and-packaging.md §6, integrity rule).
- **A — Ask.** End with a small, concrete next step. An answer without an ask is a lecture.

Standing rules: an objection is engagement — a prospect who objects is negotiating with himself. Never interrupt, never say "non, mais". Write every new objection verbatim into the CRM notes column the same day; this playbook grows from the field.

## 2. The seven objections

### 2.1 "C'est cher" / "غالي" (price)

*What it really means:* he is comparing you to software subscriptions, not to a patient's value. The math has not happened yet.

- **Acknowledge:** "Vous avez raison de compter — c'est un vrai budget, et il doit se justifier."
- **Reframe:** off software, onto one patient. Use the number HE gave you in discovery.
- **Proof:** the ROI table (pricing-and-packaging.md §4), the founding terms, the guarantee.
- **Ask:** a measured 30 days.

**Verbatim — FR:**
> « 1 000 dinars par mois, c'est la moitié d'un seul patient libyen à votre propre moyenne de [2 500]. La vraie question n'est pas le prix du service — c'est le prix des messages sans réponse. Ce mois-ci, à votre avis, il est de combien ? Et pour les cliniques fondatrices : premier mois à 350 dinars, installation offerte, remboursement intégral si la valeur n'est pas évidente. Le risque financier est de mon côté, pas du vôtre. »

**Verbatim — AR:**
> «الخدمة بألف دينار — نصف مريض واحد على حسابك إنت ([2,500]). السؤال الحقيقي موش قداش تكلف الخدمة، أما قداش يكلفوك الرسائل اللي ما يتجاوبوش. هالشهر، على رأيك قداش؟ وللعيادات المؤسِّسة: أول شهر بـ350 دينار، التركيب مجاني، والفلوس ترجع كاملة إذا القيمة ما بانتش. المخاطرة عليّ أنا، موش عليك.»

Then silence. If he keeps pushing after the founding terms, the objection is not price — it is trust; switch to §2.6. Concession floors live in pricing-and-packaging.md §8; never cross them.

### 2.2 "Patients prefer humans" / "المريض يحب يحكي مع إنسان"

*What it really means:* fear of looking cold, of damaging the clinic's reputation for care.

- **Acknowledge:** "Complètement d'accord — la médecine est humaine, et elle doit le rester."
- **Reframe:** the agent does not replace the human conversation; it replaces the silence before it. At 22:47 the choice is not human vs. assistant — it is an answer in seconds vs. nothing until 10:00. And the Libyan patient writes to three clinics at once; the first useful answer wins.
- **Proof:** the demo's escalation moment (one-pager-and-demo.md §3.1, message 5) and the takeover: the instant staff types, the assistant steps back. It also introduces itself honestly as the clinic's assistant — no pretending.
- **Ask:** a live experiment.

**Verbatim — FR:**
> « Ce soir à 22h, envoyez un message à votre propre clinique, comme un patient. Ce que vous vivrez — c'est ce que vit un patient de Tripoli chaque nuit. Puis envoyez le même message à la démo. Le patient ne choisit pas entre un humain et un assistant ; il choisit entre une réponse et le silence. »

**Verbatim — AR:**
> «الليلة على العشرة، ابعث مساج لعيادتك إنت، كيما مريض. اللي باش تعيشو، هو اللي يعيشو مريض من طرابلس كل ليلة. بعد ابعث نفس المساج للديمو. المريض ما يخيرش بين إنسان ومساعد — يخير بين إجابة وصمت. واللي يجاوب الأول، ياخذ الموعد.»

### 2.3 Data privacy / medical confidentiality / "السر الطبي"

*What it really means:* professional obligation plus fear of scandal. This objection deserves the most precise answer in the playbook — never wave it away.

- **Acknowledge:** "C'est la bonne question, et le secret médical n'est pas négociable."
- **Reframe (honest and devastating):** today the clinic's WhatsApp lives in somebody's personal phone — no access control, no log, and it walks out the door if that person resigns. Our setup is more controlled than the status quo, not less.
- **Proof:** five written commitments, shown in the contract (pricing-and-packaging.md §7):
  1. The clinic owns all conversation data and the patient list.
  2. Never sold, never shared, never reused across clients.
  3. Export on request; deletion within 30 days of termination.
  4. The assistant handles logistics only — it never collects diagnoses or gives medical opinions; clinical content is escalated to staff with a logged handoff.
  5. Every conversation is logged and auditable by the clinic, and access on our side is limited to what operating the service requires.
- **Ask:** review the data clause together at kickoff, line by line.

**Verbatim — FR:**
> « Aujourd'hui, vos conversations patients vivent dans le téléphone personnel de la personne qui tient le WhatsApp — sans journal, sans contrôle d'accès, et elles partent avec elle si elle démissionne. Notre configuration est plus contrôlée que votre situation actuelle, pas moins. Et tout est écrit : vos données vous appartiennent, jamais partagées, supprimées sous 30 jours si nous nous quittons. Je vous propose de relire cette clause ensemble au démarrage. »

**Verbatim — AR:**
> «توا، محادثات مرضاك عايشة في تليفون شخصي متاع اللي ماسك الواتساب — لا سجل، لا تحكم في الدخول، وإذا مشى، تمشي معاه. تركيبتنا أكثر انضباطاً من وضعك الحالي، موش أقل. والكل بالمكتوب: البيانات ملكك، لا تتشارك، وتتمسح في 30 يوم إذا افترقنا. نقراو البند مع بعضنا في أول جلسة.»

### 2.4 "We already have a receptionist" / "عندنا كاتبة"

*What it really means:* loyalty to staff, plus quiet fear of internal politics. Warning: the receptionist often holds the WhatsApp — she can be your champion or your assassin. Involve her from minute one of onboarding.

- **Acknowledge:** "Gardez-la — elle est précieuse. Ce n'est pas un remplacement."
- **Reframe:** the coverage math (pricing-and-packaging.md §4): she works 48 of the week's 168 hours; the assistant covers the other 120, the Sunday peaks, and the ten simultaneous chats no human can juggle. She confirms the bookings it captures; her time returns to the patients standing in front of her.
- **Proof:** the lead card — the assistant captures, the human decides.
- **Ask:** two questions to the owner.

**Verbatim — FR:**
> « Elle couvre 48 heures ; la semaine en compte 168. Qui prend les 120 restantes — les soirs, le dimanche, le mois où elle est en congé ? L'assistant capture, votre équipe confirme. Elle passera moins de temps à éteindre WhatsApp et plus de temps avec les patients au comptoir. Demandez-lui combien de conversations elle jongle à 11h un lundi. »

**Verbatim — AR:**
> «الكاتبة تخدم 48 ساعة، والجمعة فيها 168. الـ120 اللي يفضلو — الليالي، الأحد، والشهر اللي تاخذ فيه عطلة — شكون يغطيهم؟ المساعد يلقط، وفريقك يأكد. هي تربح وقتها للمرضى اللي قدامها، موش للتليفون. اسألها قداش من محادثة تلعب بيها في نفس الوقت نهار الاثنين الصباح.»

### 2.5 "Is it reliable? What if it makes mistakes?" / "وإذا يغلط؟"

*What it really means:* in medicine, an error has a face. He needs to know the failure modes are bounded.

- **Acknowledge:** "Question légitime — dans une clinique, une erreur n'est pas anodine."
- **Reframe:** the current error rate on night messages is 100% — nobody answers at all. We start from there, with guardrails no human team has.
- **Proof — the five guardrails, count them on your fingers:**
  1. Closed knowledge base: it answers only from clinic-approved content.
  2. When unsure, it says so and hands over — it never invents (demonstrated live in the demo, message 5).
  3. Clinical questions always escalate, contractually.
  4. Every conversation is logged; during week 1 of the pilot we review transcripts together daily, and corrections ship the same day.
  5. The 30-day guarantee sits under all of it.
- **Ask:** judge it on the pilot's reviewed transcripts, not on hypotheticals.

**Verbatim — FR:**
> « Le risque zéro n'existe pas — mais aujourd'hui, le taux d'erreur sur les messages de 23h est de 100 % : personne ne répond. L'assistant, lui, ne répond que depuis votre contenu validé, dit "je transmets à l'équipe" quand il ne sait pas, et chaque conversation de la première semaine est relue ensemble, corrigée le jour même. On le juge sur des transcriptions réelles, pas sur des hypothèses. »

**Verbatim — AR:**
> «الغلط وارد، كيما أي موظف جديد — أما الفرق: هو يجاوب كان من المحتوى اللي صادقت عليه، وإذا ما يعرفش يقول "نحوّلك للفريق"، وعمرو ما يخترع. وأول جمعة، نقراو المحادثات مع بعضنا كل يوم ونصلحو في نفس النهار. وتذكر: توا، نسبة الغلط في رسائل الليل 100% — ما فماش رد أصلاً.»

### 2.6 Trust / newness — "Who else uses it? You have no references" / "شكون يخدم بيها؟"

*What it really means:* he is being asked to go first. Respect that; never fake social proof.

- **Acknowledge — with full honesty:** "C'est vrai. Nous sommes au début, et je ne vais pas prétendre le contraire."
- **Reframe:** being first is exactly why the founding terms are extreme — the risk transfers to us, and the upside (locked rate, exclusivity) is his alone. And he is not trusting a foreign website: the engineer is fifteen minutes away.
- **Proof:** the demo that just answered him in three seconds — the product gives its own reference; the guarantee; the local presence; the exclusivity clause.
- **Ask:** judge on 30 measured days, not on a logo list.

**Verbatim — FR:**
> « C'est exact, et je préfère vous le dire moi-même : vous seriez parmi les cinq premières cliniques. C'est précisément pour cela que l'installation est offerte, le premier mois à 350 dinars, le remboursement intégral garanti, le tarif bloqué douze mois — et l'exclusivité de votre spécialité à [ville] pendant six mois. Le pari, c'est moi qui le prends. Vous, vous jugez sur trente jours mesurés. Et je suis à Sousse : vous m'appelez, je suis à la clinique. »

**Verbatim — AR:**
> «صحيح، وما نكذبش عليك: باش تكون من الخمسة الأوائل. وعلاش تظن التركيب مجاني وأول شهر بـ350 والضمان كامل والتعريفة مقفولة عام؟ خاطر المخاطرة نهزها أنا، موش إنت. وإنت تاخذ حصرية تخصصك في [المدينة] ستة أشهر. احكم على 30 يوم مقاسين، موش على قائمة أسماء. وأنا في سوسة — تعيطلي، نجيك للعيادة.»

### 2.7 Language / dialect accuracy — "Will it understand Libyan patients?" / "يفهم اللهجة؟"

*What it really means:* he has seen menu-chatbots die on the first message in derja.

- **Acknowledge:** "Question essentielle — le libyen n'est pas le tunisien, et aucun des deux n'est l'arabe des livres."
- **Reframe:** this corridor is exactly what the assistant is configured for: Tunisian derja, Libyan dialect, MSA, French, English — plus voice notes, which are half of real patient traffic. And when a phrase is genuinely ambiguous, it asks a clarifying question instead of guessing.
- **Proof:** the demo is the test. Hand the phone.
- **Ask:** let him try to break it.

**Verbatim — FR:**
> « Ne me croyez pas sur parole — écrivez-lui comme vos patients vous écrivent : en dialecte, en vocal, avec des fautes. Essayez de le perdre. Et si un jour une tournure libyenne lui échappe, il pose une question au lieu d'inventer, et nous l'ajustons le jour même pendant le pilote. »

**Verbatim — AR:**
> «ما تصدقنيش — جربها إنت: اكتبلو بالليبي، بالدارجة، بالغلطات، ابعثلو صوتية. حاول تضيعو. وإذا فلتتلو عبارة، يسأل باش يفهم وما يخترعش — ونعدلوها في نفس النهار وقت البايلوت.»

Extra languages (Italian, German for European cosmetic tourists): add-on at TND 150 / USD 50 per month — note it, don't pitch it uninvited.

## 4. Clinic onboarding checklist

Principle: 45 minutes of the owner's time, total. We do the heavy lifting; nothing goes live without written sign-off; the receptionist is in the room — she is the daily user and the political center of gravity.

### 4.1 Kickoff form — collect ALL of this in the 45-minute session

**A. Identity & access**
- [ ] Clinic legal + brand name, address, Google Maps pin
- [ ] WhatsApp number that will host the assistant (existing number or new dedicated one; confirm ownership — the number stays the clinic's, in writing)
- [ ] Owner/decision-maker: name, direct WhatsApp
- [ ] Lead-card & report recipient(s): name, role, WhatsApp number
- [ ] Google account email for the synced Sheet (Growth and above)

**B. Specialties & team**
- [ ] Doctors: name, title, specialty, consultation days
- [ ] Services offered — and, critically, **services NOT offered** (the #1 source of wrong answers)
- [ ] Equipment / distinctions the clinic permits mentioning

**C. Hours**
- [ ] Weekly schedule, lunch closures, weekend hours
- [ ] Public holidays, annual closure, Ramadan schedule, summer high-season adjustments

**D. Languages & tone**
- [ ] Language priority (AR incl. Libyan dialect / FR / EN; add-on languages if any)
- [ ] Tone: professional-warm default; formal register in Arabic and French; emoji policy (default: none)
- [ ] Assistant introduction line approved (e.g., "المساعد الرقمي لعيادة X" — it always identifies itself)

**E. FAQ & approved answers**
- [ ] Top 20 real questions: skim the clinic's last 50 WhatsApp threads together in the meeting — their history writes the FAQ
- [ ] Approved price table: what may be quoted as a range, what is strictly "after assessment"
- [ ] Payment methods accepted (cash, card, currencies for Libyan patients), deposit policy
- [ ] Insurance / CNAM position
- [ ] Directions, parking, nearest landmarks
- [ ] Logistical preparation instructions, ONLY in doctor-approved wording (e.g., fasting before a blood test)

**F. Medical-tourism module**
- [ ] Procedures open to traveling patients + approved indicative ranges
- [ ] Quote process: what the patient must send (existing panoramic, photos, reports — routed to the doctor, never interpreted by the assistant), promised turnaround (e.g., written quote within 24h)
- [ ] Typical stay duration per procedure (approved wording)
- [ ] Accommodation / airport-transfer partners, if any
- [ ] Documents patients should bring; facilitators/agencies the clinic works with (tag them as partners)

**G. Escalation rules** (fill the matrix below)
- [ ] Who receives clinical questions; who covers emergencies; who handles complaints; who owns price negotiation
- [ ] Takeover mechanics confirmed: the assistant pauses the moment staff writes in a thread; resumes only on staff instruction

| Trigger | Assistant action | Routed to | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical question (diagnosis, medication, suitability) | Never answers substance; logs + orientation message | Dr [name] / [number] | < 2 business hrs |
| Expressed urgency (acute pain, bleeding, "urgent") | Immediate message: call clinic [number] or 190; staff alerted | Reception + on-call doctor | Immediate |
| Complaint / anger | Apology + contact capture; no justifications | Director | < 4 hrs |
| Price negotiation beyond approved ranges | States approved range; offers a call | Reception | < 4 business hrs |
| Known facilitator / agency writes | "Partner" tag; priority notice | Owner + Youssef | < 1 hr |
| Existing patient mid-treatment asks for the doctor | Direct handoff | Secretariat | < 2 hrs |

**H. Operations**
- [ ] No-show reminder timing (Growth+; default: 24h before + 3h before, wording approved)
- [ ] Weekly report day/time and recipient
- [ ] Out-of-scope topics list (anything the clinic never wants discussed on WhatsApp)

### 4.2 Go-live gates — no launch until every box is ticked

- [ ] Knowledge base v1 signed off in writing by the owner
- [ ] Staff test: 10 scripted messages sent by reception — derja, Libyan dialect, French, one voice note, one medical trap, one emergency phrasing — all handled correctly
- [ ] Escalation test: clinical test question reached the right person's phone
- [ ] **Day-0 baseline card recorded** (required for the 30-day guarantee measurement, pricing-and-packaging.md §6): current first-response time (mystery-shop figure from target-list-strategy.md), estimated weekly inquiry volume, after-hours share, current weekly bookings via WhatsApp
- [ ] Report recipient confirmed receiving a test report

### 4.3 Timeline — kickoff to live in 5 days

| Day | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| D0 | Youssef + owner + receptionist | 45-min kickoff; form completed; content pack collected (price list, hours, last-50-threads skim) |

## 5. The 7-day pilot success plan

The contractual decision happens at day 30 (guarantee). The emotional decision happens in week 1. By day 7 we need two things: clean numbers against the Day-0 baseline, and **one undeniable save story** — a real after-hours conversation that became a booking. Get those, and day 30 is a formality.

### 5.1 Metrics — define them once, report them identically every time

| Metric | Definition | Day-7 target | Baseline source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median first-response time | Send → first substantive reply | < 10 seconds | Mystery-shop figure (often hours) |
| Inquiries handled | Patient threads answered by the assistant | 100% of inbound | Est. weekly volume, Day-0 card |
| After-hours share | % of threads starting outside opening hours | Measured (typically ~40%) | Day-0 estimate |
| Appointment requests captured | Lead cards with a requested slot | Count | Current weekly WhatsApp bookings |
| Bookings confirmed | Requests confirmed by staff | Count (staff-verified) | Same |
| Medical-tourism leads | Cards with procedure + city of origin + travel window | Count + list | Usually untracked = 0 |
| Escalations | Clinical/complaint handoffs + staff response time | 100% routed correctly | n/a |
| Voice notes understood | Vocal threads handled without human help | Count | n/a |
| Pipeline represented | Leads × clinic's own average value (V) | TND figure — label it **pipeline, not revenue** | Discovery notes |

Honesty rule: "pipeline represented" is stated as potential, never as earned revenue. Overclaiming in week 1 costs the renewal in month 3.

### 5.2 Day by day

- **Day 0 (go-live):** send the baseline card in writing. Agree what success looks like in one sentence: "Si en 7 jours il capture [X] demandes dont [Y] hors horaires, on est d'accord que ça travaille ?" Get the yes — it is the same yes that closes day 7.
- **Day 1:** watch every conversation live. Fix knowledge gaps the same day. Evening mini-report: "14 conversations, réponse médiane 6 secondes, 2 demandes de rendez-vous — captures d'écran jointes."
- **Day 2:** first correction cycle shipped. Ask reception for friction, thank her by name in the message to the owner.
- **Day 3:** mid-pilot note + hunt the save story: one screenshot of an after-hours thread that became a request. Send with one line: "Ce message est arrivé mardi 22h41. Il est maintenant une consultation vendredi."
- **Day 4:** silence toward the owner (no spam); 15-minute takeover refresher with reception.
- **Day 5:** verify no-show reminders fired (Growth+); tally the lead list.
- **Day 6:** compute metrics; assemble the Day-7 report; book the 20-minute review call.
- **Day 7:** send the report; run the review call (§5.4).

> 20 minutes [jeudi 17h] pour passer les chiffres ensemble ?

### 5.4 Day-7 review & close script

Structure — 20 minutes: frame (1') → numbers vs. baseline (5') → the save story (2') → their read (3') → corrections committed (2') → the close (5').

1. **Frame:** « Vingt minutes : les chiffres, ce qu'on corrige, et la suite. »
2. **Numbers:** read the report side by side with the Day-0 card. No adjectives — the gap between "13 heures" and "6 secondes" needs no help.
3. **The save story:** show the screenshot. Then the question that closes deals: « Avant, qu'est-ce qui lui serait arrivé, à ce message de 22h41 ? » — and let THEM say "rien". Their words, their close (same principle as pricing-and-packaging.md §9: the next voice decides).
4. **Their read:** « Qu'est-ce qui vous a surpris ? Qu'est-ce qui manque ? » Log every answer; each one is either a correction or an upsell.
5. **Corrections:** name them, date them. Reliability is a behavior, not a claim.
6. **The close — FR:**
> « Le pilote court jusqu'au jour 30 et la garantie reste entière — rien ne change ce soir. Ma question est simple : si les trois prochaines semaines ressemblent à celle-ci, vous continuez ? [oui] Alors verrouillons aujourd'hui le tarif fondateur : 800 dinars par mois au lieu de 1 000, bloqué douze mois, exclusivité [spécialité] à [ville]. Vous restez couvert par la garantie jusqu'au jour 30. On l'écrit maintenant ? »

**The close — AR:**
> «البايلوت يكمل للثلاثين يوم والضمان قائم — حتى شيء ما يتبدل الليلة. سؤالي بسيط: كان الثلاثة أسابيع الجاية يشبهو لهالجمعة، تكمل؟ [نعم] إذن نقفلو اليوم التعريفة التأسيسية: 800 دينار في الشهر عوض 1,000، مضمونة عام كامل، مع حصرية [التخصص] في [المدينة]. والضمان يغطيك للنهار الثلاثين. نكتبوها توا؟»

**Branches:**
- *"On verra au jour 30" →* « Parfait — c'est votre droit, et la garantie est là pour ça. Fixons juste aujourd'hui les critères du oui : [X demandes capturées, Y rendez-vous confirmés, zéro incident]. Si au jour 30 ces trois lignes sont vertes, on signe la suite. D'accord ? » Book the day-30 call before hanging up. Criteria agreed in advance cannot be renegotiated by a mood.
- *Lukewarm / unhappy →* no defense. List the fixes, dates, and invoke the guardrail options only if needed (pricing-and-packaging.md §8: 45-day extension against a 6-month commitment). A rescued skeptic is louder than a easy win.
- *On Pilot tier, loved the voice notes →* the day-30 upsell is Growth: « Les vocaux et la qualification complète des patients libyens, c'est le plan Growth — au tarif fondateur, 800 dinars. »

### 5.5 Day-30 renewal (short script)

Recap the 4-week cumulative report against baseline, then:

> FR : « Le contrat prévoit la suite : trois mois au tarif fondateur, puis mois par mois. Première facture le [date], par virement ou Payoneer.
