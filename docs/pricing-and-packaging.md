## 1. Pricing philosophy

We do not price against software. We price against what a clinic loses when a patient's message goes unanswered — and what it gains when every inquiry is answered in seconds, 24/7, in the patient's language.

Three rules, always:

1. **Never state the price before the value math.** First establish what one medical-tourism patient is worth to *their* clinic, in *their* numbers.
2. **Quote both currencies.** TND for local bank transfer, USD for Payoneer. FX basis: 1 USD ≈ 3.1 TND, reviewed quarterly.
3. **Anchor to one patient.** Every tier costs less per month than a single average medical-tourism patient brings in. Say this sentence in every pricing conversation.

### The leak we monetize

A typical active clinic page receives 100–400 WhatsApp inquiries per month. Model at 200:

- ~40% arrive after hours or on weekends → **80 messages** nobody answers in the moment.
- Half of those get a late reply or none at all → **40 conversations at risk.**
- If only 1 in 4 had booking intent → **10 potential bookings at risk.**
- Capture half of them → **5 recovered patients/month.**
- At TND 2,500 blended value → **TND 12,500/month (≈ USD 4,000) currently leaking.**

Even if the true number is a fifth of that — one patient — every tier below pays for itself. That is the entire pitch, and it survives skepticism.

## 4. ROI calculator

**Formula:**

> Monthly return = (N × V) ÷ M
> N = extra patients booked/month attributable to the agent
> V = average revenue per patient (TND)
> M = monthly fee (TND)

**Growth tier (M = TND 1,000) — revenue multiple of cost:**

| Extra patients/mo (N) | V = TND 1,000 | V = TND 2,500 | V = TND 6,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.0× | 2.5× | 6.0× |
| 2 | 2.0× | 5.0× | 12.0× |
| 3 | 3.0× | 7.5× | 18.0× |
| 5 | 5.0× | 12.5× | 30.0× |
| 10 | 10.0× | 25.0× | 60.0× |

**Worked example (say it out loud in meetings):**
"If the agent captures just 3 extra Libyan patients a month at your average of 2,500 dinars, that is 7,500 dinars of revenue against 1,000 dinars of cost — 7.5 times return, every month. And that ignores local patients, reduced no-shows, and your receptionist's freed-up time."

**Secondary anchor — the labor comparison:**
A receptionist covers ~48 of the week's 168 hours for TND 900–1,400/month. The agent covers all 168 hours, in three languages, for TND 500–1,000 — and it never resigns, sleeps, or puts a patient on hold. Use this as the *second* argument, never the first: we are not replacing the receptionist, we are covering the 120 hours she was never there for.

Purpose: first 3–5 logos in Sousse & Sfax, fast, with references and a case study. Limited because Youssef personally runs every onboarding — that is the honest scarcity, use it.

**Why this works:** the clinic risks TND 350 and 90 minutes of their time against a measured shot at five-figure monthly recovery. Refusing requires believing they miss *zero* messages — which the mystery-shop data (see target-list-strategy.md) usually disproves before the meeting starts.

**Integrity rule:** we have no clients yet. Never imply we do. Proof = live demo speed, the guarantee, a local engineer they can meet in person, and their own missed-message math. That is enough, and it is honest.

## 7. Contract & billing terms

- **Term:** 30-day pilot → 3-month initial term → month-to-month. 30 days written (WhatsApp) notice to cancel.
- **Billing:** setup due at kickoff; monthly in advance. USD via Payoneer invoice; TND via local bank transfer. Prices locked 12 months from signature.
- **Meta/WhatsApp fees:** patient-initiated (service) conversations carry no Meta fee. Outbound template messages (reminders, campaigns) are billed by Meta per message and passed through at cost; Growth includes an allowance of 200 template messages/month.
- **Data:** the clinic owns its conversation data and patient list. Export available on request. Deletion within 30 days of termination. We never sell, share, or reuse patient data across clients.
- **Clinical safety clause (put it in writing, it sells):** the agent provides logistical and informational answers from the clinic's approved content only. It never diagnoses, prescribes, or advises medically; clinical questions are escalated to clinic staff with a logged handoff.
- **Number ownership:** the WhatsApp number remains the clinic's. If we part ways, they keep it.
- **Support SLAs:** per tier table above.

## 8. Negotiation guardrails (internal — never show)

**You may concede:**
- Annual prepay −15% (stackable with founding rate: floor Growth TND 680/mo — hold there).
- Extended pilot to 45 days for a signed 6-month commitment.
- One free add-on for 3 months (Instagram channel is the cheapest to give).

**Never concede:**
- Monthly below the founding rate (TND 400 Pilot / TND 800 Growth). Below this, the account costs more attention than it returns and poisons referrals.
- Free setup outside the founding window — setup is real engineering work.
- Unlimited scope ("can it also do X?") without a written add-on.

**Trade, don't give.** Every discount buys something: a named case study, 2 warm introductions to other clinic owners (worth TND 300 / USD 100 referral credit each against their invoice), or prepay.
